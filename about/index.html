About

Hello, I am Odette.

I make small websites for small businesses, and I write down what I learn here.

OM

I have been building websites for fourteen years, the last six on my own. My clients are bookshops, bakeries, a harbor ferry and one very patient beekeeper. None of them want a web application. They want a page that loads, says the right thing and lets a customer get in touch.

That turns out to be a craft with real depth. Choosing a line length, wording a form label, deciding what to leave out: these are small decisions, and a good site is mostly made of them.

What this notebook is

Galley is where I keep notes on that craft. A galley is the long tray a printer sets type in, and the rough proof pulled from it before anything is final. The posts here are in that spirit. They are short, practical and open to correction.

Get in touch

If you have a question, a correction or a site that needs making, use the contact page. I answer everything, usually within a couple of days.
